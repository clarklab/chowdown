ingredients:
- Bag frozen broccoli
- Breasts of chicken
- 1/2 lb Velveeta cheese
- 1 can cream celery soup
- 1/2 cup mayo
- 1/2 cup sherry
- 1 can water chestnuts
- 1 can fr. fried onion rings


directions:
- In a greased pan, layer broccoli, then meat.
- Mix the soup, sherry, cheese, and mayo.
- Cook mix until cheese is melted.
- Pour on top, add water chestnuts.
- Bake at 350° until browned.
- Cook for 30 minutes, add onion rings.
