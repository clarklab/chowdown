yield: 2 litres

ingredients:
- 2 litres wholefull fat milk, fresh or UHT
- 100 gram powdered skimmed milk
- 85 gram commercial plain or greek style yogurt


directions:
- Boil water and sterilise 4 half litre jars, the pot you'll be using to scald the milk and the utensils you'll be using
- Put the milk in the sterilised pot and heat it to 185F / 85C and keep it there for 10 minutes, stirring constantly
- Add the powdered milk and stir to combine
- Let the milk mixture cool down to 113F / 45C
- Once the milk mixture has reached 113F / 45C, mix a ladle of it into the yogurt
- Add the yogurt-milk mixture to the pot and stir to combine. Be careful not to introduce too much air.
- Set the dehydration oven to 104F / 40C
- Pour the mixture into half litre jars. Close the jars with a silicone cover.
- Put the jars in the dehydration oven for 6-8 hours. The longer you ferment, the more sour the yogurt will be. 
- Once fermentation is complete, let the jars cool to room temperature for half an hour, then store in the fridge overnight.

---

Storage: keep in the fridge, and use clean utensils so as not to contaminate the yogurt.
