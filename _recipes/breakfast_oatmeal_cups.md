ingredients:
- 5 cup rolled oats
- 2 1/2 cup applesauce or yogurt
- 1 tsp salt
- 5 tbsp agave syrup or honey
- 2 1/3 cup water
- 1/4 cup peanut butter
- 2 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- mix-ins; raisins or other dried fruit / shredded coconut / chopped nuts / ground flaxseed


directions:
- Preheat oven to 190C. Line muffin tins or use silicone muffin cups.
- In a large mixing bowl, combine all dry ingredients well.
- In a separate bowl combine and stir all wet ingredients.
- Mix wet ingredients into dry ingredients until just combined, then fill the muffin cups until each is 2/3 full.
- Bake for ~21 minutes.
- Once the muffins have cooled down they can be frozen. If you use silicone muffin cups you can leave them in there and freeze them as is.

---

These can be frozen so you have a whole bunch of breakfasts ready to go. Defrost in the fridge or at room temp, or in the microwave on low.
